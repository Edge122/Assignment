OUR MISSION

To bring APU students together through esports, teamwork and responsible gaming.

OUR VISION

To build a welcoming esports community where players of all skill levels can learn and grow.

OUR OBJECTIVES

  • Help players improve their skills and communication.
  • Organise friendly matches and practice sessions.
  • Promote fair play, respect and healthy gaming habits.
